# Current Lotus website emblem

Active logo: public/assets/brand/lotus-emblem-256.png. Full transparent master: public/assets/brand/lotus-emblem.png.

Edited from the owner-supplied circular gold and green emblem. The Vespa was replaced with coastal hills, a bay, sunset and a phinisi boat to represent both scooter rental and City tour. The outside yellow background was removed; the green badge interior remains. Gold lettering, side 22s, lotus and water motif are retained. Generated with the built-in image editing tool. PNG app icons and ICO favicon are resized from the transparent master. Previous flower assets remain archived and are not referenced by the website.
