# Lotus flower website mark

Final assets: `public/assets/brand/lotus-flower.svg`, `public/icon-512.png`, and favicon/app-icon sizes in `public/`.

Design: seven layered gold lotus petals with three flowing, tapered water ribbons on a transparent background, without crest, scooter, circle or lettering. The website keeps the existing brand name alongside the mark.

References: the two owner-supplied lotus logo photographs from 18 November 2026.

The built-in image generation tool was used for a concept. Prompt: clean seven-petal symmetrical golden lotus, forest-green detail only if needed, no text/circle/scooter/shadow, transparent background, readable at 16px. The final website asset was redrawn as native SVG geometry; PNG and ICO sizes were rendered from the same petal geometry. The raw generated concept is not used on the website.

Instagram cards: user-supplied highlight links are placed after the itinerary. Original Instagram covers could not be retrieved; local destination photographs serve as illustrative covers. No Instagram embed scripts or autoplay are loaded.
