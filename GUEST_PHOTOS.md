# Guest photo processing

Seven owner-supplied photos processed separately using built-in imagegen edit mode. All visible faces (including staff, profiles and partial background faces) and vehicle plates were reviewed for strong baked-in privacy blur. Superimposed captions/player UI removed. Natural exposure/colour/detail improvements; AI may reconstruct fine texture. No invented names, ratings or testimonials added.

Originals: source-photos/guests/originals/guest-1.png through guest-7.png. Edited masters: source-photos/guests/edited/guest-1.png through guest-7.png. Website JPEGs: public/assets/guests/guest-1.jpg through guest-7.jpg. Only processed JPEGs are served; originals are outside public/.

Common exact prompt:

Edit this real guest handover photograph for a rental website. Preserve the real scene, people count, clothing, poses, scooters and surroundings. Modest natural exposure, white balance, denoise and sharpening improvements only; do not beautify into CGI or invent detail. PRIVACY REQUIRED: apply strong opaque-looking Gaussian privacy blur to the ENTIRE FACE of EVERY person, including staff, side profiles, partial faces and distant people. No facial eyes/nose/mouth may remain identifiable. Blur ALL vehicle registration/license plates including dates, foreground scooters and background cars; characters must be completely unreadable. Remove only superimposed captions, social/video player UI, arrows and watermarks, reconstructing natural background; keep actual shop signage and factory bike logos. No new text. Keep full original framing and aspect ratio, no collage. Output a single natural improved photo with visible privacy blurs baked into the image.
