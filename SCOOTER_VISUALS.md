Built-in imagegen edit mode, transparent output. Each model was edited separately using the owner-supplied reference (Scoopy and NMAX use existing catalogue references). AI-assisted views are illustrative, not exact measurements or a guarantee of rental colour/variant.

Originals: source-photos/scooters/originals/{model}.png
Enhanced masters: source-photos/scooters/standardized/{model}.png
Website delivery: public/assets/scooters/studio/{model}.jpg

All website assets are 1200 × 900, white background, complete silhouettes, common ground baseline at y=825. Alpha bounds are trimmed before proportional placement. Relative visual scales: Beat .84, Scoopy .88, Vario125 .92, Vario160/Stylo .94, Aerox .96, NMAX/PCX 1.00. This is an approximate visual guide, not a dimensionally calibrated comparison. Prices are unchanged.

## Exact shared prompt

Create a standardized scooter catalogue product-photo cutout by EDITING the supplied target reference, not inventing a different scooter. Full motorcycle in a consistent front-right three-quarter view, front wheel on the RIGHT of the frame, rear on LEFT, visible exhaust side. Camera at approximately headlamp height with a modest downward angle, a 70mm catalogue lens, 30 degrees towards the front from a side-on view. Entire scooter visible including mirrors, both wheels and fenders. Wheels straight, upright on a center stand, tire contact points on one level baseline. Preserve the target model's exact body panels, headlamp shape, wheel design, trim, original paint colours, seat colour and factory decals/logos. Keep all manufacturer badges and decals attached to the bike. Remove only the surrounding promotional captions, price labels, ribbons such as BARU/CBS/ABS callout graphics, and all original background. Clean high-resolution natural studio lighting, neutral colour balance, soft even highlights, no stylization or design upgrades. Output one single isolated scooter on a genuinely TRANSPARENT background with no ground plane, no backdrop, no rectangle, no checkerboard and no cast shadow. Minimal equal transparent padding around the complete silhouette, subject centered and filling about 85% of a landscape 3:2 canvas. Target dimensions around 1536x1024. Match this same camera direction, framing and lighting across the series. Do not add accessories or change variant.
